The Q4 Moment Menu

September 28 to December 31, 2026. Pick three to five big moments and three or four trend days. Not everything.

Big moments · pick three to five

  • Halloween · Sat Oct 31
  • Veterans Day · Wed Nov 11
  • Thanksgiving · Thu Nov 26
  • Black Friday · Fri Nov 27
  • Small Business Saturday · Sat Nov 28
  • Cyber Monday · Mon Nov 30
  • Giving Tuesday · Tue Dec 1
  • Hanukkah · Dec 4 to 12
  • Green Monday · Mon Dec 14
  • Super Saturday · Sat Dec 19
  • Christmas · Fri Dec 25
  • New Year's Eve · Thu Dec 31
Two things to notice. Thanksgiving through Giving Tuesday is six days in a row: that is one campaign with six doors, not six campaigns. And Christmas is a Friday, so the last real shopping weekend is December 19 and 20.

Trend days · pick three or four

Not campaigns. Single posts. One photo, two sentences, fifteen minutes.

  • Intl Coffee Day · Thu Oct 1
  • Dessert Day · Wed Oct 14
  • Boss's Day · Fri Oct 16
  • Sweetest Day · Sat Oct 17
  • Sandwich Day · Tue Nov 3
  • Kindness Day · Fri Nov 13
  • Entrepreneurs Day · Tue Nov 17
  • Espresso Day · Mon Nov 23
  • Cookie Day · Fri Dec 4
  • Ugly Sweater Day · Fri Dec 18

Dates that matter only to my business

Your anniversary, a local festival, a school calendar, a seasonal peak. Often worth more than any national holiday, because nobody else is competing for that day.

The filter: run every date through these four

  1. Does my customer care about this date? Not do I. Do they.
  2. Can I deliver if it works?
  3. Do I have the lead time? Count backward.
  4. Is this authentically mine?
