## What changed, and what it means for you

**We are no longer making flyers or videos today.** The afternoon used to be three production sprints. It is now the fourth work module plus a peer pressure-test of the calendar. The reason is simple: a business owner who leaves with one flyer and no plan has nothing in December. One who leaves with a dated plan and no flyer can make the flyer any evening in twenty minutes.

Two consequences to say out loud:

1. **The weekly rhythm on slide 36 is what their plan prescribes, not what they do today.** Three posts and one video a week, starting this week, from the calendar Claude builds them. Do not let anyone think they are meant to produce it in the room.
2. **The asset-making is a future session.** Say that explicitly at slide 3 and again at slide 40, so nobody feels shortchanged. They are getting the harder half today.

## What to protect if you fall behind

In order of what to cut:

1. **Topic 1.** Slides 10 and 11 can go to two minutes each. It is a refresher.
2. **A case study.** Talk over it in thirty seconds instead of three minutes.
3. **The Moment Draft.** Four volunteers instead of the whole room.

**The channels section is Topic 3, slides 20 to 25, and it now has its own work block.** If you are short on time there, cut slides 21 and 22 to about a minute each and protect slide 23, Found Build Accelerate. That single slide is the whole idea, and the first three questions of Module 04 depend on it.

**Never cut Module 04 or Module 05.** Module 04 is where the message gets made, and without it there is no campaign, just a list of dates. Module 05 is the thing they take home. If it comes to it, run Module 04 short and let them finish it tonight, but do not skip starting it in the room.

**There is also a new downloadable calendar**, October through January, with more than sixty dates and a play for each one. It lives on Module 03. Point at it when you teach Topic 4, because it answers every "but what about..." question in the room.

## Room size adjustments

**Five or six people.** Every share-out becomes one circle. Sit beside people during the work blocks.

**Fifteen or more.** Name a table captain per table. Share-outs happen at the table, not to the room. You circulate to captains.

**Anyone without a Master Brain.** Send them to Module 00b on the hub. Announce it once to the whole room at 9:15 so nobody has to admit they lost their file. They run about twenty minutes behind and catch up over lunch.
